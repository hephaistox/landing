# Automatons

More, and more, I see automatons like fully independent projects, demonstrating optimization aspects.

## Automaton's philosophy

To solve an optimization problem, the first step is to analyze the real life problem and decide what to solve, and how to solve it. The point is to decide among all existing optimization technologies which one to use. The design of automatons makes it easy to quickly express a modelling and have some feedback of the optimization problem.

To illustrate this. Let's consider a decision taken in the real life. The modeller should decide if:
- that decision is modelled or neglected
- if that decision is explored with the metaheuristic, and with what neighborhood - knowing that the neighborhood is essential for optimization efficiency and neighborhood choice is tighly coupled with the existence of the problem.
- if it should be an heuristic during the evaluation process,
