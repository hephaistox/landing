Operations research

Before Hephaistox, six years of research in scheduling, carried out on real industrial cases (PSA, Michelin, Aubert & Duval). Here is what remains of it in writing.

PhD thesis

Le problème de jobshop avec contraintes : modélisation et optimisation

PhD in operations research (in French) · Clermont-Ferrand (now Université Clermont Auvergne) · 2006

How to schedule a workshop when reality adds its own constraints to the textbook model: minimum and maximum delays between two operations, transport of parts from one machine to another. The thesis models these constraints and proposes optimisation methods to solve them.

Read the thesis on HAL

International journal articles

Two articles published in leading operations research journals, drawn from the thesis.

Computers & Operations Research · 2008

A memetic algorithm for the job-shop with time-lags

A. Caumond, P. Lacomme, N. Tchernev · vol. 35 (7), p. 2331–2356

A memetic algorithm (genetic algorithm and local search) for the job-shop with minimum and maximum time-lags between operations.

Read the article (DOI 10.1016/j.cor.2006.11.007)

European Journal of Operational Research · 2009

An MILP for scheduling problems in an FMS with one vehicle

A. Caumond, P. Lacomme, A. Moukrim, N. Tchernev · vol. 199 (3), p. 706–722

A mixed integer linear program to schedule a flexible manufacturing system where a single vehicle carries the parts.

Read the article (DOI 10.1016/j.ejor.2008.03.051)
